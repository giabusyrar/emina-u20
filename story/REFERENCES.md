# Reading notes — 10 references before writing "H-1"

What each book does that works on Indonesian teens, and the one thing we steal from it.
(Desk research, Nov 2026: reviews, academic analyses, reader threads. Links at the bottom.)

| # | Reference | What makes it land | What we steal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Dilan 1990**, Pidi Baiq | Short, unexpected dialogue. Simple, almost-formal Indonesian with twisted phrasing that turns into quotable lines. Humor from understatement, never from jokes. | Nay and Dimas get Dilan lines: short, dry, slightly off. "Kalau aku pingsan, kamu yang ngomong." The best lines live in dialogue, never in narration. |
| 2 | **Mariposa**, Luluk HF | Youthful jokes spread across every character, code-mixing, a persistent female lead who acts instead of waiting. School life as the whole world. | The player always ACTS. Every side character gets at least one joke. Rara, Kinan, Dimas, Sasa each have a voice. |
| 3 | **Nanti Kita Cerita Tentang Hari Ini**, Marchella FP | Short snippets organised by time of day (Pagi, Siang, Sore, Malam). Shareable, relatable, screenshot-sized. | Scenes carry a time chip (07.10 → 18.45). Every ending closes on one short, shareable sentence. |
| 4 | **Geez & Ann / Kata**, Rintik Sedu | Plain words, heavy meaning; melancholic monologue. Readers also complain it gets too sad and unreal. | Melancholy is rationed to the Deep Talk lane (tangga belakang) and never more than two lines in a row. |
| 5 | **Perahu Kertas**, Dee Lestari | Passion vs. expectation. Two creative kids who believe in a dream. Hope as the engine. | The thematic spine for Made to Explore: the day is about finding how you move, not what you should be. Welcome copy comes from here. |
| 6 | **Hujan**, Tere Liye | One motif (rain) threaded through everything; first-person intensity; a late twist. | Rain is foreshadowed at 07.10 (BMKG screenshot), pays off at 17.15 (blackout), resolves at 18.45 (lights on). |
| 7 | **Antares**, Rweinda | Written at 16. Sixty percent casual register, real slang, code-mixing, a strong female lead. Authentic because it is not translated from adult speech. | Keep the slang that teens actually type: seen doang, gercep, santuy, FINALLY, caps lock for panic. Drop anything that sounds like a brand pretending. |
| 8 | **Dear Nathan**, Erisca Febriani | Angst and over-reactions as truthful teen behaviour; the friend group as comic chorus; formal/informal mixed in dialogue. | Nay over-reacts in caps, and that is a feature. The committee is the chorus. |
| 9 | **Garis Waktu**, Fiersa Besari | Poetic closer at the end of each short piece, social-media rhythm, contrast as the dominant device. | One closer per ending, placed last, under ten words. Used once per ending, not per scene (stop-slop rule). |
| 10 | **Laskar Pelangi**, Andrea Hirata | Ensemble friendship, laughter and tears in the same page, an underfunded school event (the 17 Agustus carnival) won by the kid everyone underestimated. | The pensi is our carnival. Kinan (dekor) and Sasa (kelas sepuluh, gitar) are the underestimated ones who carry the night. |
| + | **Oxenfree / Life is Strange** (interactive) | Choices read like conversation: what you say or do, when, or whether you stay silent. Consequences are social, not mechanical. | Options are verbs in the first person ("Diem, dengerin sampe dia selesai"), silence is a valid choice, and the reaction line is always about a person, never a score. |

## Rules derived for this script

1. Dialogue carries the humor. Narration stays plain and short (one or two sentences per beat, under 26 words).
2. Each scene has a named person in it with one want. No anonymous "cewek yang gulung kabel".
3. The player chooses actions, phrased the way she would say them in her head.
4. One running motif: rain. One running object: the HT (walkie talkie) handed over at 17.15.
5. Stakes are personal and concrete: Nay's name on the poster, Kinan wanting to be seen, Sasa never having played for anyone.
6. Endings happen the morning after and end on a short line that works as a caption.
7. Voice: aku/kamu throughout (Emina register), slang allowed in dialogue, caps lock allowed for Nay only.
